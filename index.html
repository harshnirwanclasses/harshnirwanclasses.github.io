About Harsh Nirwan Classes

Online coaching for Physical Education teacher recruitment exams — KVS & NVS TGT/PET, DSSSB PET/TGT, UP TGT and UP PGT Physical Education, UP Assistant Professor and UGC NET Physical Education. Study with video lectures, downloadable PDF notes, topic-wise MCQ practice and full-length mock tests in Hindi and English.

Topics covered

History & foundations of PE, Olympic movement, anatomy & physiology, exercise physiology, kinesiology & biomechanics, sports training, sports psychology, test & measurement, health education & nutrition, sports injuries & first aid, yoga, rules of games and sports, organisation & administration, and teaching methods.
